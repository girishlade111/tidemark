 Your rights
 This page explains how Tidemark collects, uses and protects your information. Questions about anything here can go to legal@tidemark.co.
 01
 Who we are
 Tidemark Ltd is a performance marketing agency registered in England and Wales, with offices in London and Lisbon. We are the controller of the personal data described here.
 02
 What we collect
 Contact details you send us through forms or email, such as your name, work email, company and role.
 Account access you grant us for client work, such as ad platforms and analytics, which we only use to deliver the agreed services.
 03
 How we use it
 To reply to enquiries, deliver audits and client services, send our weekly newsletter if you subscribe, and improve our website.
 04
 Cookies and analytics
 We use privacy friendly analytics and only set non essential cookies with your consent. You can change your choice at any time from the cookie banner.
 05
 Sharing and transfers
 We never sell your data. We share it only with processors who help us run the business, such as email and hosting providers, under written agreements.
 06
 Your rights
 You can ask to access, correct, export or delete your data at any time by emailing privacy@tidemark.co. We reply within 30 days.
